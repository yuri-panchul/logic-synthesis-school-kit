Additional rules, update the testbenches and the style guide as well.

Look at
https://github.com/yuri-panchul/basics-graphics-music/blob/main/labs/4_microarchitecture/4_2_fifo/4_2_4_flip_flop_fifo_with_counter_view_1_functional/tb.sv
* Use new SystemVerilog syntax to specify timescale, not old `timescale.

* Use "wire" if and only if it is a continuous assignment "wire a = ...". Otherwise use "logic".

* Align "=" vertically in a sequence of assignments.

* Do not change clock in the middle of "initial" block with blocking assignment. Isolate it into:

logic clk = 1'b0;
always # (period / 2) clk = ~ clk;

* Do not use "# delay" unless necessary.
Usually you need only "@ (posedge clk)"
and sometimes "@ (posedge clk); #1" if you have combinational logic after a register and you want to sample it.

* Never drive or sample anything on negedge unless you have to.

* Do not use begin/end in tasks in Verilog-95 style. SystemVerilog allows to omit them.

* Testbench timeouts should be counted in clock cycles, not in absolute "# delay".

Look at the following testbenches as examples:

https://github.com/yuri-panchul/basics-graphics-music/blob/main/labs/4_microarchitecture/4_2_fifo/4_2_4_flip_flop_fifo_with_counter_view_1_functional/tb.sv

https://github.com/verilog-meetup/systemverilog-microarchitecture-challenge-for-ai-2/blob/main/testbench.sv

If you cannot follow it for whatever reason, write an HTML report need_to_deviate_from_style.html to explaining why.
