Issues Codex is confident about

I have not established a failure in the first XOR or 4013 demonstrations that requires the added HCT buffer, clock conditioner, external LED resistors or capacitors.

Those additions have been removed from the normal instructions and kit inventories. This file records specific, supported functional constraints; it does not turn general design recommendations into proven failures of your demonstrations. Unverified concerns are in the separate uncertainty file.

The automatic adder must also check result-queue capacity

In your reference module, lines 74–79 combine both operand-valid signals with the result queue’s readiness. The wrapper, lines 28–34, turns valid/ready into push/pop. Therefore pop_A = pop_B = push_SUM = !empty_A & !empty_B & !full_SUM. If A/B were consumed while SUM was full, those operands would be lost without storing their result. This is a definite functional error in such an implementation, not evidence of a need for analog support components.

A 40105 pulse interface is not the RTL’s clocked interface

The selected LSSK row 36 part is CD74HC40105E. Its manufacturer datasheet, page 2 specifies a rising SI edge for loading and a falling SO edge for removal. DIR means input-ready and DOR means output-valid. The reference FIFO accepts push/pop on a common positive clock edge. Simply relabelling CLK/PUSH/POP as SI/SO is not an equivalent connection.

For lamps explicitly labelled EMPTY and FULL, EMPTY = !DOR and FULL = !DIR after flags settle. Directly labelling DOR as EMPTY or DIR as FULL reverses their meaning. The inventories budget source-listed AND/NOT gates for the required Boolean and polarity functions. The precise pulse-routing netlist is a separate unverified implementation detail; no specific extra capacitor, conditioner or storage stage is established as necessary here.

Master reset initializes the FIFO’s valid-word state. Its data outputs do not represent a valid word while DOR is low. These are device semantics, not evidence of a particular measured failure.

The seven-segment exception is explicitly in your course

Habr lesson 1.2 and LSSK row 51 specify 560 Ω resistors for the bare seven-segment display used with CD4511. Those remain only in the corresponding exercises, with one per driven segment. They are separate from the already current-limited individual LEDs and LED bars used in the first lessons. Course, lesson 1.2 · CD4511 reference.

Other definite function / quantity corrections

  • The full three-FIFO project needs three FIFO packages; the original workbook’s quantity of one does not cover it.
  • The July three-display demonstration needs three display drivers and three displays.
  • CD4077 contains four two-input XNOR gates, not three-input gates. No extra components are needed to correct that description.
  • CD4019 has shared Ka/Kb controls. A single package does not supply four independent per-bit mask controls. The catalogue distinguishes shared vector selection from the basic-gate arbitrary-mask variant.
  • One CD4086 provides one four-term AND-OR-INVERT function. An eight-term selection needs more than one such function.
  • A CD4511 is a BCD decoder, not a hexadecimal decoder. The source’s DM9368 alternative has different wiring.

None of these facts establishes a failure rate for your demonstrated circuits or justifies putting the removed support parts back into the beginner lesson.
