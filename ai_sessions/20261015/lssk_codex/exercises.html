How to use the counts. Each entry is a complete, estimated setup for one student or pair. Reuse parts between exercises. M–M and M–F counts are working allowances, including rails, fixed-input patch leads and signal wiring; kits add 25% and round up to 40-lead packs. Each LED bar contains eight current-limited LEDs. Button counts mean individual resistor-equipped modules. Classroom USB power and a multimeter are shared equipment.

The kit uses the specified 5 V LEDs, resistor-equipped buttons and assembled clock modules. The new single-FIFO variant adds the four TTP223 switches requested in the review. Counts are estimates for the named circuits; see sources and counting assumptions.
