Exercises

  • G1 Getting started: breadboard, power, 5V LEDs, a gate chip and buttons
  • B1 Demo the basic gates 4081 (AND), 4071 (OR), 4069 (NOT), 4070 (XOR)
  • B2 De Morgan's laws
  • B4 4013 D flip-flop with asynchronous reset
  • B5 4029 up/down binary/BCD counter
  • B6 4015 serial-in / parallel-out shift register
  • B7 Seven-segment display and the 4511 decoder
  • B8 4008 4-bit adder with buttons and the LED array
  • B9 4008 adder with three 4511 decoders and three displays

Also possible with the parts in this kit: M1 Half adder from XOR and AND, M2 Full adder from basic gates, M3 2-bit adder from a half adder and a full adder, M10 Johnson counter from 4015 and 4069, P5 FSM that recognizes the sequence 01 (the Harris & Harris snail).

Wires and buttons

The largest build needs about 49 wires on the breadboard and 4 between boards and 24 male-female jumpers. With about 25% spare, that is 2 × 40 Dupont M-M jumpers. Each button module and the 555 module take three M-F jumpers.

Compared with the sheet

The LSSK sheet has a Quality basics column for this kit. Differences:

  • The sheet's 'basics' column has one 4511, one display and no 560 Ω resistors; the July 31 basics list asks for three 4511s and three displays for the 4008 exercise (B9), so this kit has three, plus the 22 segment resistors they need.
  • The sheet's column has a 4072 (dual 4-input OR) but no 4071; the exercise list names the 4071, so this kit has the 4071.
  • The sheet's column has one breadboard; B9 with three displays does not fit on one 830-point board, so this kit has two.
  • A second pack of 5 buttons is added for the 8-input adder (the sheet notes that it is needed for adder and comparator exercises).
  • One single 5V-tolerant LED is added for the first 'try it' step of the getting-started notes.
