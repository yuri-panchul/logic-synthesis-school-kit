Variant A or B

Both variants build BB8 and P3 with the same buttons, LEDs, clock module and boards. They differ in what happens while a button is held. In variant A, each press gives one clean edge on SI or SO through a 4013 flip-flop, so one word moves per press. Variant B adds glue logic that makes the 40105 behave like the clocked SystemVerilog FIFO that students write next on the FPGA (lab 4_2_5 of basics-graphics-music), so the breadboard and the RTL can be compared clock by clock.

Compared with the sheet

The LSSK sheet has a Quality FIFO column for this kit. Differences:

  • The sheet's 'FIFO' column has no 40105, no 4008, no clock module and no M-F jumpers for the buttons; this kit adds all of them.
  • Two 4013s and a 4082 replace the second 4081: flip-flops synchronise the push and pop buttons and make the transfer pulse, because a clock-gated AND of the ready flags moves several pairs in one clock tick.
  • The sheet's 2 x 4511 and the 4-button LED module are listed as optional here: LED modules and button modules are enough for the exercises.
