Compared with the sheet

The LSSK sheet has a Quantity all column for this kit. Differences:

  • Two 4008s instead of one (M4, the 8-bit adder, cascades two).
  • Three 40105s instead of one (P3, the FIFO adder, uses three).
  • Two 4086s instead of one (M6/M8: an 8-input mux needs two 4-wide AND-OR-INVERT gates).
  • The HEX decoders, 40108 and 40181 are listed as optional: the 4511 covers the display exercises, and the two rare chips are only needed for BB9 and BB10.
  • One more 830-point breadboard: P1 and P2 need three large boards.
  • Three single 5V-tolerant LEDs (red, green, yellow) for the getting-started notes and the FIFO status outputs.
