# Logic Synthesis School Kit

From AND/OR gates on a breadboard to your own CPU on an FPGA. Breadboard exercises with CMOS 4000 chips, the parts they need, and five kits with costs and suggested prices.

[**How to start**First circuit in five steps, English](getting_started_en.html) [**Как начать**Первая схема за пять шагов, по-русски](getting_started_ru.html) [**39 exercises**Gates to FIFOs, arbiters and FSMs, with parts and wire counts](exercises.html) [**FSM task cards**A different code-lock sequence for every student](kit5_fsm.html#tasks)
