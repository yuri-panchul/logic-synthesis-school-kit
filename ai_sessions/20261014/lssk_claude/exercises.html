Breadboard exercises

All 39 small-scale-integration breadboard exercises from the July 31 and July 23 Verilog Meetup posts and from Habr section 1 (lessons 1.1-1.7), merged where they overlap, with the components each one needs from the LSSK sheet.

How the counts were made. Each exercise uses the kit's simplifications: 5V-tolerant LEDs and 8-LED arrays without resistors, button modules with built-in pull-downs (VCC / OUT / GND), an assembled 555 clock module, and the breadboard power module set to 5V. Only 7-segment displays need 560 Ω ½ W resistors.
  • Pre-cut wires are jumpers on the breadboard: two per chip for power, one per signal connection, and one per tie-off. CMOS inputs must never float, so every unused input of a used chip is tied to GND or +5V. These are estimates from the chip pinouts (±20%); the kits add about 25% spare.
  • Dupont M-F: 3 per button module and 3 for the 555 module (VCC, signal, GND), as in note 5.
  • Dupont M-M: power rails between two or three breadboards.
  • Exercises are built one after another, so a kit needs the maximum of each part over its exercises, not the sum.

The student draws the state diagram, encodes the states, derives the next-state equations and builds them with gates and a 40174. Solving all 120 tasks with 2-5-bit sequences shows that one task needs up to 3 state flip-flops (+1 to synchronize the button), up to 8 gate chips and 34-98 wires; the gate assortment below covers every one of them. The FSM kit page generates the task cards and the answer keys.

4076 or 4013 can replace the 40174.

July 31 post: ProjectsHabr 1.7

Two flip-flops hold GT and LT; every clock takes one bit of A and of B, LSB first: GT' = A & ~B | GT & (A xnor B), LT' = ~A & B | LT & (A xnor B), EQ = NOR(GT, LT) on the traffic light. Variants: MSB first, cascaded 4063s, 4 bits per clock. Compare with BB3.
