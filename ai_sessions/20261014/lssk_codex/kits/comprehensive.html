One exercise is assembled at a time; the inventory is the maximum quantity of each component across those exercises, not the sum of all exercises.

In the box: 4 full-size boards, 10 button modules, 3 LED bars (24 LEDs), 3 seven-segment displays, 240 M–M and 80 M–F jumpers. Additional indicators, support parts and tools are itemized below.

Includes the rare parts. CD40108 and CD40181 are budgeted at the spreadsheet’s historical $28.84 and $19.78 each, even though its quantity cells are zero. Availability is unverified. This is a complete curriculum inventory proposal, not a claim that a ready-to-ship kit is currently procurable. Custom FSM coverage is bounded to 2–4 input bits; open-ended CPU projects are outside the defined breadboard list.
